Frequently asked

How precise are the positions? Luna uses Jean Meeus’ Astronomical Algorithms: VSOP87 for the Sun and planets, ELP-2000/82 for the Moon. Positions are apparent, tropical and geocentric, with light-time, aberration, nutation and ΔT. Lunation times agree with published tables to within seconds.

Which void-of-course rule is used? By default the traditional rule: the Moon is void from its last exact Ptolemaic aspect (conjunction, sextile, square, trine, opposition) to the Sun through Saturn until it enters the next sign. Settings can add Uranus, Neptune and Pluto.

Which time zone are times shown in? Your device’s time zone, shown on the Now and Calendar screens.

Why didn’t I get a notification? Check that notifications are on for Luna in iOS Settings. Open Luna now and then so it can schedule the next alerts.
