Privacy policy

Effective October 24, 2026

Luna does not collect any data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your device

All astronomical calculations run on your iPhone or iPad. Your settings (such as the void-of-course rule and which notifications you want) are stored only on your device.

Notifications

Notifications are scheduled locally by the app. No server is involved and nothing is sent anywhere.

Third parties

Luna does not share data with anyone, because it does not collect any.

Children

Luna is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here. Questions: open a request.
